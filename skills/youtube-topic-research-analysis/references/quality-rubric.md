# 多源主题深度解读质量规范

## Corpus gate

- Use at least three videos from at least three channels; five is the default target.
- Confirm every transcript was read to EOF.
- Reject sources that are only tangential, duplicative, promotional, or too incomplete.
- Identify shared upstream evidence. Five channels repeating one press release are not five independent confirmations.
- Preserve useful role diversity without forcing artificial balance.

## Cross-validation gate

For every major claim, distinguish:

- independent agreement;
- shared-origin repetition;
- partial agreement under different definitions or scope;
- direct disagreement;
- single-source assertion;
- report-level inference.

Do not decide truth by vote count. Weight primary observation, transparent measurement, reproducibility, incentives, scope, and recency.

The published evidence table needs at least five substantive claim rows. Its confidence label must follow the prose rather than replace it.

## First-principles pillars

Use 6–9 pillars. Each one must:

1. state a durable conclusion rather than a topic;
2. begin from an actual philosophical, causal, institutional, or epistemic origin;
3. contain at least four meaningful transitions;
4. explain why every transition follows;
5. integrate source agreement or disagreement where relevant;
6. remain distinct from other pillars;
7. land in a concrete system or decision.

Use one `哲学原点`, `推演主线`, and `最小命题` per pillar. A chapter below roughly 750 Unicode characters usually lacks causal explanation; do not pad it.

## Epistemic discipline

- Attribute company performance, numerical claims, forecasts, and self-reports.
- Do not promote repeated claims into facts without independent support.
- Separate disagreement about facts from disagreement about values, definitions, or time horizons.
- Treat a missing counterexample as uncertainty, not proof.
- Use external primary sources only when needed to verify an unstable or consequential claim; cite them directly.

## Viewpoint crop and insights

The crop must remove views, click-through packaging, authority cues, repeated anecdotes, financing, demos, and timing predictions while preserving transferable structure.

Write 5–8 insights. Each must combine at least two pillars and normally two independent sources. Reject any insight that merely renames a chapter.

## Action framework

Write 6–10 ordered actions. Each needs a verb-led decision, a reason grounded in the synthesis, and an observable completion condition. Move from problem definition to evidence gathering, implementation, validation, governance, and iteration.

## Readability

- Write a coherent article, not a literature-review log.
- Make paragraphs carry the reasoning; use lists only for truly parallel material.
- Use source names selectively.
- Avoid repetitive “A says / B says” sentence chains.
- Remove timecodes, search scores, ranking narration, process notes, placeholders, and stray untranslated connective prose.
- Retain useful technical English when translation would reduce precision.
- Follow `ai-terminology.md`; `Agent` word-family terms must not be flattened into “代理” compounds.

## Forbidden scaffolding

Reject:

- `第一步` / `第二步` / `第三步`
- `通读全文` / `第一性原理提取`
- `把问题还原到不可简化的层次`
- `节目锚点` / `支点观点`
- `可审计的论证链`
- `边界与反证`
- `没有充分回答的问题`
- `节目时间结构`
- `支柱一` / `核心观点一` 等数字化章节前缀
- “由于上下文限制……”
- “这些视频都提到了，所以可以证明……”

## Calibration

The accepted single-source corpus uses 7–9 pillars, 6–7 insights, 8–9 actions, and roughly 12,400–14,500 Unicode characters. A five-source synthesis will often be 14,000–20,000 characters because it must explain evidence relationships. This is a depth signal, not a padding target.

## Final manual gate

Answer yes to all:

1. Can a reader reconstruct the topic's global causal structure?
2. Does the report represent every selected source's major contribution and late-stage qualifications?
3. Are shared-origin repetition and independent support clearly distinguished?
4. Are genuine disagreements preserved rather than averaged away?
5. Does every pillar derive rather than summarise?
6. Are the insights new cross-source combinations?
7. Does the evidence table match the prose?
8. Can practitioners act and verify progress?
9. Is every unnecessary meta sentence gone?
10. Does every high-signal AI term preserve the source's professional meaning and remain consistent throughout?
